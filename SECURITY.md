# Security policy

Kastle Five builds privacy and security tools, so a report of a weakness in our own work is welcome and taken seriously.

## What this covers

- **K5**, the developer workspace, and **KryWire** and **Kry4help**: [kastlefive.dev](https://kastlefive.dev)
- **ActivePray**, the iPhone app, and [activepray.com](https://activepray.com), including its calendar feeds and the prayer-times widget
- The repositories of [Kastle Five](https://github.com/Kastle-Five)

## How to report

Email **kastlefive@icloud.com**, with "Security" in the subject. Please include:

- what you found and where (an address, a version, a screen);
- how to reproduce it, step by step;
- what an attacker could do with it, as far as you know.

Please don't open a public issue for a security problem. We aim to reply within a week, to say what we will do and when, and to tell you once it's fixed. If you would like to be credited, say how.

## Testing in good faith

We will not take legal action over research that:

- touches only your own accounts and test data, and stops as soon as it reaches anyone else's;
- doesn't degrade a service (no denial-of-service or load testing, no spam);
- uses no social engineering or physical access;
- gives us reasonable time to fix a problem before it's made public.

There is no paid bug bounty.

Contact details are also published in [security.txt](https://activepray.com/.well-known/security.txt), as RFC 9116 describes.
